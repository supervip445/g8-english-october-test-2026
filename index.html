I

Reading Comprehension

20 Marks

Our Environment

Our environment is very important to us. It provides us with air, water, food and shelter. People, animals and plants are living things, while air, water and soil are non-living things.

However, human activities are causing many environmental problems. People cut down trees, burn fossil fuels and throw chemical waste into rivers and seas. These activities cause air pollution, water pollution and climate change.

We should protect our environment. We should reduce the amount of waste we produce. We should reuse things instead of throwing them away and recycle old newspapers, plastic bottles and other useful materials. If everyone works together, we can protect our planet for future generations.

A. Answer the following questions. 5 × 2 = 10

  1. Why is the environment important to us?
  2. What are some living things mentioned in the passage?
  3. What are three harmful human activities?
  4. What environmental problems do these activities cause?
  5. What are the three Rs?

B. Write True or False. 5 × 1 = 5

  1. Plants are living things.
  2. Soil is a living thing.
  3. Burning fossil fuels can cause environmental problems.
  4. We should throw everything away after using it once.
  5. Everyone should work together to protect our environment.

C. Find words from the passage which mean: 5 × 1 = 5

  1. very important = ______
  2. things people do = ______
  3. dirtying the air = ______
  4. use something again = ______
  5. people who will live in the future = ______

Answer Key

  1. It provides us with air, water, food and shelter.
  2. People, animals and plants.
  3. Cutting down trees, burning fossil fuels and throwing chemical waste into rivers and seas.
  4. Air pollution, water pollution and climate change.
  5. Reduce, Reuse and Recycle.

True/False: 1. True   2. False   3. True   4. False   5. True

Vocabulary: important; activities; air pollution; reuse; future generations.

Writing

A. Paragraph Writing 8 Marks

The Most Helpful Person in My Family

Write a paragraph of about 100–120 words. Include:

  • Who is the person?
  • What is he/she like?
  • What does he/she do?
  • How does he/she help others?
  • Why do you like/adore him/her?

B. Email Writing 7 Marks

Invitation to a Camping Trip

Write an email to your friend inviting him/her to a camping trip. Include the destination, date, activities, camping equipment and invitation. Your friend should accept or decline the invitation.

I'd love to join you. • I'm really looking forward to it. • Thanks for your invitation. • I'm afraid I can't. • I hope I can join you next time. • Yours / All the best

Sample Answers

A. Paragraph

The most helpful person in my family is my mother. She is kind, patient and responsible. She always takes care of our family and helps me with my schoolwork. She also cooks delicious meals for us and keeps our home clean. When I have a problem, she listens to me and gives me good advice. She works hard every day but she always has time for our family. I really admire and love my mother because she is always ready to help others. She is the person I can depend on whenever I need help.
